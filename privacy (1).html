Clipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Who we are

Clipper is operated by JHP, Vienna, Austria. Contact: legal-clipper@proton.me.

2. What data we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive from TikTok:

  • Your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar)
  • An access token and refresh token that allow Clipper to upload content on your behalf

We also store the video clips, titles and settings you create in Clipper.

3. How we use it

We use this data only to show which account content will be posted to and to upload the clips you approve. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where it is stored

All data is stored locally on our own computer/server. Tokens are kept confidential and are not shared with anyone.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as your TikTok account is connected. When you disconnect Clipper or ask us to delete your data, we delete the tokens and associated data within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to its processing. You can also file a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde). To exercise your rights, email us at the address above.

7. Disconnecting

You can revoke Clipper's access at any time in the TikTok app under "Security" → "Manage app permissions".

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

See also our Terms of Service.
